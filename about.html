Who We Are

Mazenod Community Connect was created to support the Mazenod Wellness Campaign, a community event designed to encourage healthy living, fitness and positive communication among residents. The campaign focuses on bringing people together through wellness activities, health education and community engagement programs.

The event helps residents learn more about physical health, mental wellness and healthy eating habits. Community members can participate in fitness sessions, wellness discussions, health awareness programs and outdoor activities aimed at improving the quality of life in Mazenod.
